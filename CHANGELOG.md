# SENSEI Presentation Changelog

## March 5, 2026 - Unified Belt Criteria Update

### Major Changes

#### 1. Unified Belt Progression (All Associates)
- **Previous**: Separate criteria for PAs and AMs with team UPH requirements
- **Updated**: Single unified standard based on individual UPH performance
- **Rationale**: Individual UPH is the consistent metric; leadership actions drive team UPH but aren't directly measured

#### 2. Reduced Total Hours
- **Previous**: 250 hours to Black Belt
- **Updated**: 100 hours to Black Belt
- **Impact**: More achievable progression timeline while maintaining rigor

#### 3. Individual UPH Baseline System
- **New**: Baseline established at White Belt entry using 4-week rolling average
- **New**: Q1 rate adjustment (+1.9%) applied to all baselines
- **New**: Relative improvement thresholds (+5%, +10%, +12%, +15%, +18%, +20%)
- **Benefit**: Fair for both PAs and AMs regardless of floor time

#### 4. Updated Belt Requirements

| Belt | Hours (Old) | Hours (New) | UPH Criteria (Old) | UPH Criteria (New) |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| White | 10 hrs | 5 hrs | 37.16+ | Baseline engagement |
| Yellow | 40 hrs | 20 hrs | 42.73+ | ≥ LP baseline |
| Orange | 70 hrs | 35 hrs | 44.59+ | +5% above baseline |
| Green | 100 hrs | 50 hrs | 46.45+ | +10% above baseline |
| Blue | 140 hrs | 65 hrs | 46.45+ | +12% above baseline |
| Purple | 180 hrs | 75 hrs | 48.31+ | +15% above baseline |
| Brown | 220 hrs | 90 hrs | 50.17+ | +18% above baseline |
| Black | 250 hrs | 100 hrs | 50.17+ | +20% above baseline |

#### 5. Quality Yield Adjustments
- **Previous**: 92%+ starting threshold
- **Updated**: 90%+ starting threshold (White Belt)
- **Progression**: Gradual increase from 90% to 98% across all belts
- **Emphasis**: Must be sustained over 30-day rolling window

#### 6. Knowledge Assessment Clarity
- **White–Orange**: Virtual quiz (10-15 questions, 80% passing)
- **Green–Purple**: In-person assessment with scenarios (Brown/Black Belt administered)
- **Brown–Black**: Comprehensive assessment + live floor observation

#### 7. Certification Authority
- **Brown Belts**: May certify White and Yellow Belt candidates
- **Black Belts**: Full certification authority across all belt levels
- **Requirement**: All certifications logged in FCLM/LMS

#### 8. Core Program Rules Added
- Process hours reset after 30 consecutive days of inactivity
- Associates may request pause/hold during extended indirect assignments (OM approval)
- Peak and non-peak hours weighted equally
- New hire dilution periods (first 90 days) flagged but do not disqualify
- Alternative metrics available for AMs without direct processing time
