What Is SENSEI?

A performance-based belt progression system built for CRET leaders.

SENSEI rewards what actually matters — not how long you've been here, but how well you perform, how deeply you understand the operation, and how many leaders you develop along the way.

Three Core Principles:

  • ⚖️ Pure Meritocracy — Advance based on what you achieve, not your tenure
  • 📊 Data-Driven — All criteria pulled from live operational systems — objective and transparent
  • 🤝 Mentorship-Driven — Higher belts require developing other leaders with measurable impact
⬜🟨🟧🟩 🟦🟪🟫⬛

Who Can Participate?

✅ Eligible: All Process Assistants (L3) and Area Managers (L4+) actively processing in CRET or AR/WHD paths at PHX6.

✅ What Counts Toward Your Hours

  • Direct processing time in CRET and AR/WHD paths only
  • Hours accumulate from your very first qualifying shift
  • 30-day inactivity rule: if you leave path for 30 consecutive days, your hour clock pauses

❌ What Does NOT Count

  • Indirect support hours
  • Administrative time
  • Time in non-CRET process paths

Performance Levels: White, Yellow, Orange

The On-Ramp. Approachable. Earned.

These three levels are about one thing: showing up and performing. No knowledge tests. No projects. Just consistent, high-quality processing that proves you belong at the next level.

⬜ White Belt (10 hrs)

Hit LP Standard. Zero incidents. That's it. Your first Peccy pin is closer than you think.

🟨 Yellow Belt (40 hrs)

15% above standard, sustained. Prove it wasn't a fluke.

🟧 Orange Belt (70 hrs)

20% above standard. Lead one safety talk. You're starting to understand why the process works, not just how to run it.

Knowledge Levels: Green and Blue

The Knowledge Gate Opens at Green.

🟩 Green Belt (100 hrs)

The first real milestone. 100 hours of qualifying performance in a demanding environment is genuinely earned.

  • Pass the cumulative knowledge assessment (CRET process paths, defect ID, quality yield drivers, basic TPH)
  • Deliver one structured OJT session to a developing leader with a documented, measurable outcome
  • Passing score: 80%

🟦 Blue Belt (140 hrs)

You are not just performing - you are owning quality outcomes.

  • Pass the advanced assessment (adds site TPH fundamentals, throughput levers, backlog management)
  • Lead one quality improvement project - minimum 1% yield improvement or equivalent documented impact
  • Begin formal mentorship of at least one leader below Green Belt

Leadership Levels: Purple and Brown

Your Impact Is Measured by the Performance of Others.

🟪 Purple Belt (180 hrs)

You understand the full CRET flow. Now you are building the next generation.

  • Pass the TPH deep dive assessment (adds AR/WHD integration, VRET impact, peak/non-peak planning)
  • Design and deliver a SENSEI training module - reviewed and approved by Sr. Ops
  • Formally mentor 2+ leaders; at least 1 must advance a belt level under your guidance

🟫 Brown Belt (220 hrs)

You are shaping the site's CRET performance culture.

  • Pass the capstone assessment (adds end-to-end TPH mastery, bottoms-up planning, cross-functional dependencies)
  • Lead a cross-functional improvement project (2+ departments) with documented business impact
  • Recognized mentor with a track record of multiple belt-level advancements
  • Eligible to administer SENSEI assessments - in person or virtually, including cross-site

The Standard: Black Belt

You Are the Standard.

⬛ The Black Belt is not a destination. It is a commitment to sustaining and elevating excellence indefinitely.

To earn Black Belt, you must:

  • Sustain ≥ 50.17 UPH and ≥ 98% quality yield over 120 consecutive days
  • Architect and implement a site-wide initiative with measurable CRET performance impact
  • Develop at least one leader to Purple Belt or above
  • Deliver a live TPH Optimization Strategy Presentation to Sr. Ops or above - 15-20 minutes, with data, proposed solution, and success metrics

The first Black Belt at PHX6 will design their own Peccy pin - a permanent symbol of what it means to reach the highest standard this program recognizes.

Mentorship Framework

Higher Belts Are Earned by Developing Others.

Mentorship is not optional at Blue Belt and above - it is a core advancement criterion.

What mentorship looks like in SENSEI:

  • Structured check-ins (weekly or bi-weekly)
  • Documented OJT sessions with measurable outcomes
  • Mid-cycle progress reviews tied to belt criteria
  • Advancement verification co-signed by mentor and manager

Mentorship documentation is tracked in the SENSEI Mentorship Template - submitted to the Program Owner upon mentee advancement.

The multiplier effect: A Black Belt who develops one Purple Belt, who develops two Blue Belts, who each develop two Green Belts - that is one leader creating a performance culture that touches 7+ developing leaders simultaneously.
